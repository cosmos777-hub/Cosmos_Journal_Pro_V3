# 01. Purpose

Coach is not defined by its interface.

It is defined by its business model.

The purpose of this document is to describe that model independently from any implementation detail.

Every recommendation, every Mission, every Playbook and every progression generated by Coach originates from the concepts described here.

This document intentionally ignores:

* UI
* Storage
* Framework
* Components
* Technical implementation

It focuses exclusively on the business language shared by Product, Design and Development.

---

# 02. Domain Overview

Coach transforms trading experience into structured progression.

Unlike the Journal, which records events, or Analytics, which explains them, Coach operates on behaviors.

Its domain is organized around a progressive transformation pipeline.

```text
Trade
    │
    ▼
Observation
    │
    ▼
Pattern
    │
    ▼
Mission
    │
    ▼
Playbook
    │
    ▼
Progress
    │
    ▼
Habit
    │
    ▼
Skill
    │
    ▼
Achievement
    │
    ▼
Digital Twin
```

Each entity has one responsibility.

No entity duplicates another.

This separation keeps the domain simple, explainable and extensible.

---

# 03. Domain Philosophy

Coach is built around one fundamental belief:

**Progress is created by improving behaviors, not by chasing results.**

For this reason, the domain never models profits as progression.

Instead, it models behaviors.

Every entity contributes to the same objective:

Transform repeated behaviors into durable skills.

The domain therefore follows a natural progression.

Facts become observations.

Observations become patterns.

Patterns become priorities.

Priorities become actions.

Actions become habits.

Habits become skills.

Skills become the evolving identity of the trader.

---

# 04. Domain Boundaries

The Coach domain begins where Analytics ends.

Analytics explains.

Coach decides.

Coach never calculates indicators.

Coach never stores trades.

Coach never predicts markets.

Coach consumes validated analytical information and transforms it into coaching decisions.

Likewise, Coach does not execute trading actions.

Its responsibility ends once a coaching strategy has been defined.

Execution always belongs to the trader.

---

# 05. Core Entities

The Coach domain is composed of a small number of entities.

Each entity owns one clear responsibility.

Together they define the complete coaching lifecycle.

---

## 5.1 Observation

### Definition

An Observation represents a single objective fact extracted from one or more completed trades.

It is the smallest analytical element within the Coach domain.

Observations never interpret.

They only describe.

### Responsibilities

* Capture measurable facts.
* Remain objective.
* Feed Patterns.

### Examples

* Manual exit detected.
* Risk exceeded.
* Setup respected.
* Trading plan followed.
* Emotional delta increased.

### Observation never...

* creates Missions;
* gives advice;
* evaluates priorities.

Its role is limited to recording coaching-relevant facts.

---

## 5.2 Pattern

### Definition

A Pattern is a repeated behavior identified from multiple Observations.

Patterns represent the first level of interpretation inside the Coach domain.

Unlike Observations, Patterns are meaningful.

They answer one question:

**"What behavior is emerging?"**

### Examples

* Frequent manual interventions.
* Excellent London session discipline.
* Repeated risk violations.
* Strong emotional stability.
* Consistent execution quality.

### Responsibilities

* Aggregate Observations.
* Detect behavioral trends.
* Measure confidence.
* Estimate impact.
* Feed the Decision Engine.

### Attributes

Each Pattern owns:

* Confidence
* Frequency
* Trend
* Impact
* Evolution

These attributes evolve continuously as new Observations arrive.

### Pattern never...

* generates Playbooks;
* communicates with the user;
* decides priorities.

It simply represents an identified behavior.

---

## 5.3 Mission

### Definition

A Mission is the highest-priority behavioral objective selected by the Decision Engine.

It is not a diagnosis.

It is a coaching decision.

Only one Mission may be active at any time.

### Responsibilities

* Focus the trader.
* Define one improvement objective.
* Activate one Playbook.
* Measure completion.

### Examples

* Reduce manual interventions.
* Improve risk discipline.
* Strengthen London execution.
* Improve emotional consistency.

### Mission never...

* explains statistics;
* analyses trades;
* replaces the trader's judgement.

Its purpose is exclusively to guide progression.

---

---

# **5.4 Playbook**

## Definition

A Playbook is the execution strategy attached to a Mission.

It transforms one coaching objective into a sequence of practical actions that can be applied naturally during future trading sessions.

A Playbook never tells the trader what to trade.

It teaches the trader **how to improve the targeted behavior**.

---

## Responsibilities

A Playbook is responsible for:

* translating a Mission into concrete actions;
* defining progressive exercises;
* proposing measurable objectives;
* monitoring behavioral progression;
* supporting skill acquisition.

---

## Characteristics

Every Playbook is:

* focused on one Mission;
* progressive;
* explainable;
* measurable;
* repeatable.

Its purpose is to facilitate execution.

Never to increase complexity.

---

## Examples

Mission

> Reduce Manual Exits

Playbook

* Wait 30 seconds before any manual close.
* Review the trading plan before overriding it.
* Record the reason for every manual intervention.
* Complete ten compliant trades.

---

Mission

> Improve London Discipline

Playbook

* Trade only during the planned session.
* Respect every entry criterion.
* Stop after the predefined number of trades.

---

## Playbook never...

* changes trading rules;
* predicts the market;
* replaces the trader's decision-making.

It supports behavior.

Nothing else.

---

# **5.5 Progress**

## Definition

Progress measures the evolution of the current Mission.

It represents the distance between the initial behavior and the desired behavior.

Progress does not evaluate profitability.

It evaluates behavioral improvement.

---

## Responsibilities

Progress is responsible for:

* measuring improvement;
* monitoring consistency;
* detecting stagnation;
* detecting regression;
* determining Mission completion.

---

## Characteristics

Progress is continuous.

It evolves after every relevant Observation.

It can:

* increase;
* stabilize;
* decrease.

This evolution reflects behavioral consistency rather than isolated events.

---

## Progress never...

* defines new Missions;
* grants Achievements;
* creates Skills.

Its responsibility is measurement.

---

# **5.6 Habit**

## Definition

A Habit represents a behavior that has become sufficiently consistent to be considered stable.

Unlike Progress, which reflects a temporary evolution, a Habit represents behavioral regularity.

It marks the transition between learning and mastery.

---

## Responsibilities

Habit is responsible for:

* consolidating repeated behaviors;
* validating consistency;
* preparing Skill acquisition.

---

## Characteristics

A Habit:

* develops progressively;
* can strengthen over time;
* can weaken if abandoned;
* is continuously reassessed.

Habits are living entities.

They evolve with the trader.

---

## Habit never...

* appears instantly;
* depends on one successful trade;
* exists without repetition.

Consistency is its only foundation.

---

# **5.7 Skill**

## Definition

A Skill is a validated capability acquired through sustained behavioral consistency.

Skills represent durable competencies.

They define what the trader is now capable of doing reliably.

---

## Responsibilities

A Skill is responsible for:

* representing mastered behaviors;
* enriching the Digital Twin;
* serving as long-term progression markers.

---

## Examples

* Risk Discipline
* Emotional Stability
* London Specialist
* Execution Precision
* Patience

---

## Characteristics

Skills:

* are durable;
* evolve slowly;
* remain explainable;
* can continue to mature.

Unlike Habits, Skills are considered part of the trader's identity.

---

## Skill never...

* depends on one Mission;
* disappears after one mistake;
* measures profitability.

It reflects competence.

Not performance.

---

# **5.8 Achievement**

## Definition

An Achievement recognizes a significant milestone reached by the trader.

Achievements celebrate progression.

They do not define it.

---

## Responsibilities

Achievements are responsible for:

* recognizing accomplishments;
* increasing motivation;
* reinforcing positive behaviors.

---

## Characteristics

Achievements are:

* optional;
* permanent;
* behavior-oriented;
* symbolic.

They never influence the Decision Engine.

---

## Examples

🏅 London Specialist

🏅 Discipline Master

🏅 Emotional Consistency

🏅 Plan Guardian

🏅 Perfect Execution

---

## Achievement never...

* unlocks features;
* modifies Missions;
* changes coaching priorities.

Recognition must never interfere with coaching.

---

# **5.9 Digital Twin**

## Definition

The Digital Twin represents the long-term behavioral identity of the trader.

It is not a dashboard.

It is not a profile page.

It is the living memory of acquired competencies.

Everything that becomes durable eventually contributes to the Digital Twin.

---

## Responsibilities

The Digital Twin is responsible for:

* preserving Skills;
* representing long-term evolution;
* exposing behavioral strengths;
* supporting future coaching decisions.

---

## Characteristics

The Digital Twin evolves slowly.

It reflects months of progression rather than individual trading sessions.

Its purpose is not to display recent performance.

Its purpose is to answer one question:

**Who is this trader becoming?**

---

## Digital Twin never...

* stores individual trades;
* calculates statistics;
* evaluates daily performance.

Its responsibility is identity.

# **06. Entity Relationships**

The Coach domain is intentionally linear.

Each entity prepares the next one.

No entity bypasses another.

```text
Trade
        │
        ▼
Observation
        │
        ▼
Pattern
        │
        ▼
Mission
        │
        ▼
Playbook
        │
        ▼
Progress
        │
        ▼
Habit
        │
        ▼
Skill
        │
        ▼
Achievement
        │
        ▼
Digital Twin
```

Although represented as a linear flow, the domain continuously feeds back on itself.

New trades generate new Observations.

New Observations refine existing Patterns.

Patterns may confirm, adjust or replace the active Mission.

Progress therefore remains dynamic throughout the trader's journey.

---

## Relationship Principles

The following principles govern every relationship inside the domain:

* A **Trade** may generate multiple **Observations**.
* Multiple **Observations** contribute to a single **Pattern**.
* A **Pattern** may trigger one **Mission** when its confidence is sufficient.
* A **Mission** always activates exactly one **Playbook**.
* A **Playbook** continuously updates **Progress**.
* Sustained **Progress** leads to a **Habit**.
* Mature **Habits** become **Skills**.
* Significant **Skills** may unlock **Achievements**.
* Every confirmed **Skill** enriches the **Digital Twin**.

Ces relations constituent la colonne vertébrale du domaine Coach. Elles resteront stables même si les algorithmes, les règles métier ou l'interface évoluent.
