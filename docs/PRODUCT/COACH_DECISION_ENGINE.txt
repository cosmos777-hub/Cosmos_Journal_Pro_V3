**Purpose**

Define the complete decision-making architecture of the Coach module.

This document specifies how Coach transforms analytical information into coaching decisions.

It defines the reasoning process, decision rules, priorities, safeguards and evolution principles of the coaching engine.

It intentionally excludes UI implementation and technical details.

---

# 01. Purpose

The Coach Decision Engine exists to answer one question:

> **"What is the single most valuable behavior the trader should improve next?"**

Every recommendation generated by Coach ultimately answers this question.

The engine does not optimize profitability directly.

It optimizes behavioral progression.

Its objective is to continuously transform trading experience into sustainable skills through transparent and explainable decisions.

---

# 02. Design Philosophy

The Decision Engine has not been designed as an algorithm.

It has been designed as a coaching reasoning process.

Its purpose is not to automate trading.

Its purpose is to emulate the thought process of an experienced mentor.

Every decision therefore follows the same natural sequence.

Observe.

Understand.

Evaluate.

Prioritize.

Coach.

Consolidate.

This sequence remains constant regardless of future technical implementations.

---

# 03. Coaching Decision Cycle

Every coaching decision follows the same immutable lifecycle.

```text
Trades
    │
    ▼
Observation
    │
    ▼
Pattern Detection
    │
    ▼
Confidence Evaluation
    │
    ▼
Priority Selection
    │
    ▼
Mission Assignment
    │
    ▼
Playbook Activation
    │
    ▼
Progress Monitoring
    │
    ▼
Skill Consolidation
    │
    ▼
Digital Twin Update
```

This cycle represents the complete reasoning model of Coach.

No recommendation bypasses any step.

---

# 04. Decision Pipeline

The Decision Engine is composed of five true decision engines followed by four execution systems.

## Decision Engines

* Observation Engine
* Pattern Engine
* Confidence Engine
* Prioritization Engine
* Mission Engine

These components create decisions.

---

## Execution Systems

* Playbook System
* Progress Tracking
* Skill Consolidation
* Digital Twin Synchronization

These components execute and monitor decisions.

They never decide.

This distinction is fundamental.

---

# 05. Observation Engine

## Responsibility

Transform analytical outputs into structured coaching observations.

The Observation Engine is the entry point of Coach.

It consumes validated information produced by Analytics.

It never interprets behavior.

It simply records coaching-relevant facts.

---

## Input

Examples include:

* Risk violation
* Manual exit
* Session respected
* Emotional instability
* Rule compliance
* Strategy consistency

---

## Output

Each detected fact becomes an Observation.

Observations are objective.

They contain no recommendation.

No priority.

No judgement.

---

## Design Principles

The Observation Engine follows three rules.

### Objective

Observations are factual.

Never subjective.

---

### Atomic

Each Observation represents one measurable fact.

Never multiple conclusions.

---

### Independent

Observations never depend on previous coaching decisions.

They only depend on validated trading data.

---

# 06. Pattern Engine

## Responsibility

Identify recurring behaviors from multiple Observations.

Patterns are the first level of interpretation inside Coach.

Unlike Observations, Patterns describe behaviors rather than isolated facts.

---

## Pattern Detection

The engine continuously groups compatible Observations.

Examples:

Repeated manual exits

↓

Pattern

Manual intervention tendency

---

Repeated emotional instability

↓

Pattern

Emotional inconsistency

---

Repeated London discipline

↓

Pattern

London execution strength

---

## Pattern Attributes

Every Pattern continuously evaluates:

* Frequency
* Stability
* Trend
* Behavioral Impact
* Confidence

These attributes evolve over time.

Patterns are therefore living entities rather than static conclusions.

---

## Pattern Evolution

Patterns may:

* emerge;
* strengthen;
* stabilize;
* weaken;
* disappear.

Coach therefore reflects behavioral evolution rather than fixed labels.

---

# 07. Confidence Engine

## Responsibility

Determine whether a detected Pattern is sufficiently reliable to justify coaching.

This engine protects Coach against false positives.

Its objective is not to maximize recommendations.

Its objective is to maximize trust.

---

## Confidence Sources

Confidence is never calculated from a single variable.

It emerges from multiple dimensions.

For example:

* observation frequency;
* behavioral consistency;
* temporal persistence;
* data quality;
* repetition across market conditions.

The precise scoring mechanism may evolve.

The philosophy must never change.

---

## Confidence States

Every Pattern belongs to one of four confidence levels.

### Emerging

Insufficient evidence.

Observation continues.

---

### Growing

Behavior becoming visible.

Monitoring increases.

---

### Confirmed

Enough evidence exists.

Pattern may become a Mission.

---

### Stable

Behavior is well understood.

Future coaching decisions may rely on it.

---

## Confidence Principle

Coach prefers delaying a recommendation over making a wrong recommendation.

False positives damage trust more than delayed coaching.

---

---

# **08. Prioritization Engine**

## Responsibility

The Prioritization Engine determines which confirmed Pattern deserves the trader's full attention.

Its purpose is not to identify every possible improvement.

Its purpose is to identify **the most valuable improvement**.

Coach deliberately sacrifices breadth in favor of focus.

---

## Design Philosophy

A trader can improve dozens of behaviors.

He cannot improve dozens of behaviors simultaneously.

For this reason, Coach intentionally limits itself to a single active priority.

The Prioritization Engine continuously evaluates every confirmed Pattern and answers one question:

> **"If the trader improves only one behavior today, which one will create the greatest long-term progression?"**

---

## Evaluation Dimensions

Every confirmed Pattern is evaluated through multiple dimensions.

### Behavioral Impact

How much does this behavior influence long-term trading quality?

---

### Improvement Potential

How much progress could realistically be achieved?

---

### Urgency

Does this behavior require immediate attention?

---

### Stability

Has the Pattern remained sufficiently consistent over time?

---

### Strategic Value

Will improving this behavior positively influence other behaviors?

---

These dimensions are intentionally independent.

A highly frequent Pattern is not automatically the highest priority.

---

## Priority Principle

Coach always prioritizes leverage over frequency.

The most common behavior is not necessarily the most important one.

The best Mission is the one capable of improving the trader as a whole.

---

# **09. Mission Engine**

## Responsibility

The Mission Engine transforms one prioritized Pattern into one coaching Mission.

This is the moment where analysis becomes action.

Everything before this point answers:

> **What is happening?**

Everything after this point answers:

> **What should we improve next?**

---

## One Mission Principle

Only one Mission may be active.

This rule is absolute.

Coach never asks the trader to divide attention between multiple objectives.

Focus creates consistency.

Consistency creates habits.

Habits create skills.

---

## Mission Creation

A Mission is created only when all conditions are satisfied.

The associated Pattern is:

* Confirmed
* Relevant
* Stable
* High priority

Only then may the Mission Engine activate coaching.

---

## Mission Replacement

A Mission is never replaced impulsively.

Changing direction too often destroys progression.

Therefore, Coach introduces controlled inertia.

An active Mission remains active unless:

* it has been completed;
* its Pattern disappears;
* a significantly more valuable Pattern emerges.

Mission switching is therefore exceptional.

Not continuous.

---

## Mission Completion

A Mission ends when one of three situations occurs.

### Success

The targeted behavior has improved sufficiently.

The Mission is completed.

---

### Transformation

The behavior evolves into a new coaching opportunity.

The current Mission naturally transitions into another.

---

### Abandonment

The Mission becomes irrelevant because the underlying Pattern no longer exists.

In this case the Decision Engine reassesses the entire domain.

---

# **10. Playbook System**

## Responsibility

Transform a Mission into an actionable progression plan.

The Playbook System never creates Missions.

It operationalizes them.

---

## Design Philosophy

A Mission defines **what** should improve.

A Playbook defines **how** it can improve.

This separation guarantees flexibility.

Future versions may replace Playbooks without modifying the Decision Engine.

---

## Structure

Every Playbook contains:

* Objective
* Behavioral explanation
* Practical actions
* Success indicators
* Completion criteria

Every Playbook remains deterministic.

Version 1 never generates Playbooks dynamically.

They are curated, reviewed and validated by the product team.

This guarantees consistency and explainability.

---

## Playbook Principle

A good Playbook should always feel achievable.

Its purpose is not to impress.

Its purpose is to change behavior.

---

# **11. Progress Tracking**

## Responsibility

Continuously evaluate the evolution of the active Mission.

Progress Tracking does not decide.

It observes whether coaching is producing measurable behavioral change.

---

## Progress Dimensions

Progress is evaluated through several complementary dimensions.

* Consistency
* Frequency
* Improvement Trend
* Regression Detection
* Completion Readiness

Together these dimensions provide a reliable picture of behavioral evolution.

---

## Progress Philosophy

Progress is never linear.

Temporary regressions are expected.

Coach therefore evaluates trajectories rather than isolated events.

A single mistake never invalidates sustained improvement.

---

# **12. Skill Consolidation**

## Responsibility

Determine when repeated behavioral improvements have become durable competencies.

Skill Consolidation represents the transition from temporary success to permanent capability.

---

## Consolidation Conditions

A Skill emerges only when:

* the Habit remains stable;
* improvement persists over time;
* behavior becomes naturally repeatable;
* regression risk becomes low.

Skills cannot be manually awarded.

They must emerge naturally from evidence.

---

## Consolidation Principle

Coach rewards consistency.

Never isolated excellence.

The purpose is to recognize lasting transformation rather than exceptional moments.

---

# **13. Achievement Recognition**

## Responsibility

Celebrate meaningful progression without influencing coaching decisions.

Achievements exist to reinforce motivation.

Never to manipulate behavior.

---

## Recognition Philosophy

Recognition follows progression.

It never replaces progression.

Achievements therefore remain symbolic.

They do not modify:

* priorities;
* Missions;
* Decision Engine logic.

---

## Recognition Principle

Coach celebrates who the trader is becoming.

Not simply what the trader has achieved.

---

# **14. Digital Twin Synchronization**

## Responsibility

Synchronize confirmed Skills with the Digital Twin.

This component preserves the trader's long-term behavioral identity.

---

## Synchronization Rules

Only validated Skills may enrich the Digital Twin.

Temporary Progress never modifies identity.

This distinction protects the stability of the trader profile.

---

## Synchronization Philosophy

Identity evolves slowly.

Coach therefore updates the Digital Twin conservatively.

Trust is built through long-term consistency.

Not recent performance.

---

---

# **15. Explainability Model**

## Responsibility

Every coaching decision must be understandable.

Coach never asks the trader to blindly trust the system.

Instead, every recommendation should be accompanied by a clear and concise explanation.

Explainability is not an optional feature.

It is a core property of the Decision Engine.

Without explainability, coaching becomes arbitrary.

Without trust, coaching loses its value.

---

## Explainability Pipeline

Every Mission should be traceable back to its origin.

```text
Mission

↓

Selected Pattern

↓

Supporting Observations

↓

Trading Data
```

The user should always be able to understand:

* what behavior was detected;
* why it matters;
* why it has become the current priority;
* how the proposed Playbook addresses it.

Coach never hides its reasoning.

It exposes it in a language adapted to the trader.

---

## Levels of Explanation

Coach progressively adapts the amount of explanation.

### Summary

A short coaching sentence.

Ideal for daily use.

---

### Detailed Explanation

A concise behavioral analysis.

Explains why the Mission exists.

---

### Full Evidence

Complete traceability.

Pattern.

Observations.

Supporting metrics.

Timeline.

This level is primarily intended for advanced users.

---

## Explainability Principle

A recommendation that cannot be explained should never be generated.

Transparency always comes before sophistication.

---

# **16. Time Model**

## Responsibility

Coach reasons over time.

Behavior cannot be evaluated from isolated events.

Every decision therefore considers temporal evolution.

---

## Temporal Philosophy

The engine observes tendencies.

Not moments.

Temporary anomalies are expected.

Long-term consistency matters far more than short-term fluctuations.

---

## Temporal Layers

Coach simultaneously evaluates several horizons.

### Immediate

Recent behavioral changes.

Used to detect emerging Patterns.

---

### Short-Term

Current Mission evolution.

Measures coaching effectiveness.

---

### Medium-Term

Habit consolidation.

Confirms behavioral stability.

---

### Long-Term

Skill evolution.

Digital Twin growth.

Identity transformation.

---

Each layer serves a different purpose.

Together they produce a stable coaching system.

---

## Temporal Stability

The Decision Engine deliberately avoids reacting too quickly.

Behavior requires time.

Progress requires repetition.

Identity requires persistence.

Coach therefore introduces natural inertia into every decision.

---

# **17. Decision Rules**

The following rules are immutable.

They define the behavioral integrity of Coach.

---

## Rule 1

A Mission must always be supported by a confirmed Pattern.

---

## Rule 2

A Pattern must always be supported by Observations.

---

## Rule 3

Observations must always originate from validated trading data.

---

## Rule 4

Only one Mission may be active.

---

## Rule 5

Playbooks never generate Missions.

They execute them.

---

## Rule 6

Skills emerge from evidence.

Never from isolated achievements.

---

## Rule 7

Achievements never influence Decision Engine priorities.

Recognition remains independent.

---

## Rule 8

Digital Twin evolves only from validated Skills.

Never from temporary Progress.

---

## Rule 9

Coach never makes trading decisions.

Its responsibility ends at behavioral coaching.

---

## Rule 10

Every recommendation must remain explainable.

If explainability is lost,

the recommendation must not exist.

---

# **18. AI Extension Model**

## Purpose

Artificial Intelligence is considered an enhancement layer.

Never a decision layer.

The Decision Engine remains the sole authority responsible for coaching decisions.

---

## AI Responsibilities

Future AI capabilities may include:

* rewriting recommendations;
* adapting coaching tone;
* generating educational examples;
* simplifying explanations;
* personalizing communication.

These capabilities improve the experience.

They never modify coaching logic.

---

## AI Limitations

Artificial Intelligence cannot:

* create Missions;
* replace Patterns;
* alter priorities;
* modify Confidence;
* override Decision Rules.

These responsibilities remain exclusive to the deterministic engine.

---

## AI Principle

**AI enhances intelligence.**

**It never replaces judgment.**

---

# **19. Engine Invariants**

The following invariants must remain true regardless of future implementations.

---

### Coaching Before Technology

The coaching philosophy always has priority over technical convenience.

---

### Behavior Before Performance

Coach optimizes behaviors.

Not profits.

---

### Trust Before Automation

Reliable coaching is more valuable than frequent coaching.

---

### Focus Before Quantity

One excellent Mission is better than ten average recommendations.

---

### Explainability Before Complexity

If increasing complexity reduces transparency,

complexity must be rejected.

---

### Stability Before Reactivity

Coach prefers stable progression over rapid adaptation.

---

### Human Before Algorithm

The trader always remains responsible for market decisions.

Coach supports.

The trader decides.

---

# **20. Closing Notes**

The Decision Engine is the reasoning core of Coach.

It transforms information into progression.

It does not seek to maximize activity.

It seeks to maximize relevance.

Every observation.

Every Pattern.

Every Mission.

Every Skill.

Exists for one purpose:

Helping the trader become progressively more disciplined, more consistent and more autonomous.

This engine is intentionally deterministic.

Not because deterministic systems are simpler.

But because trust requires predictability.

Future technologies may enrich the coaching experience.

They may improve communication.

They may personalize explanations.

They may make Coach feel more human.

But they will never replace the principles defined in this document.

Because the true intelligence of Coach does not reside in artificial intelligence.

It resides in the quality of its reasoning.
