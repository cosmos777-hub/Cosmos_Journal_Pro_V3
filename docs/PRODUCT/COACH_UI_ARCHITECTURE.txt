**Purpose**

Define the user experience architecture of the Coach module.

This document specifies how the intelligence defined by the Domain Model and the Decision Engine is presented to the trader.

It focuses on user experience, information hierarchy and interaction principles.

It intentionally avoids implementation details.

---

# 01. Purpose

Coach exists to transform behavioral intelligence into daily action.

Its interface is not designed to display information.

It is designed to maintain momentum.

Every screen, every component and every interaction should encourage the trader to continue progressing.

The objective is not to create an impressive dashboard.

The objective is to create an environment where the next meaningful action is always obvious.

---

# 02. UX Philosophy

Coach follows one fundamental principle.

> **The Mission is the Interface.**

Everything else is secondary.

Statistics.

Achievements.

Progress bars.

History.

Digital Twin.

These elements support the Mission.

They never compete with it.

The user should never wonder:

> "Where should I look?"

The interface should naturally answer:

> "Here is what matters now."

---

## Action Before Information

Coach never displays information without purpose.

Every visible element must help the trader:

* understand;
* decide;
* improve;
* stay motivated.

Information without action is considered visual noise.

---

## One Cognitive Goal

At any moment, Coach should require only one mental objective.

Complete the Mission.

Everything else contributes to that objective.

This principle minimizes cognitive load and maximizes behavioral consistency.

---

# 03. User Journey

Each visit to Coach follows the same progression.

```text
Arrival

↓

Current Mission

↓

Recommended Action

↓

Playbook

↓

Progress

↓

Reflection

↓

Exit
```

The journey is intentionally short.

Coach is designed to accompany trading.

Not interrupt it.

---

## Session Continuity

The interface should always feel persistent.

The user does not "open Coach."

The user **returns to an ongoing progression**.

The current Mission is therefore the natural entry point of every session.

---

# 04. Information Hierarchy

Coach organizes information according to behavioral importance.

Not according to technical importance.

The hierarchy remains constant.

## Level 1

Current Mission

The primary focus.

Immediately visible.

Impossible to miss.

---

## Level 2

Playbook

Concrete actions.

Visible immediately after the Mission.

---

## Level 3

Progress

Evidence that improvement is occurring.

---

## Level 4

Achievements

Recognition.

Motivation.

Celebration.

---

## Level 5

Digital Twin

Long-term identity.

Always accessible.

Never dominant.

---

## Level 6

Historical details

Available when needed.

Hidden by default.

---

# 05. Screen Architecture

The Coach screen is organized around five functional zones.

```text
Mission

↓

Playbook

↓

Progress

↓

Identity

↓

History
```

Each section naturally answers one question.

Mission

> What should I improve?

---

Playbook

> How should I improve it?

---

Progress

> Am I improving?

---

Identity

> What am I becoming?

---

History

> Why did Coach reach this conclusion?

The trader never has to search for meaning.

The layout itself communicates the progression.

---

# 06. Workspace Architecture

Like the Journal and Analytics, Coach adopts a stable workspace.

The frame remains fixed.

Content scrolls internally when necessary.

This preserves spatial memory and reinforces consistency across Cosmos.

The workspace becomes the visual anchor of the coaching experience.

Unlike Analytics, however, Coach prioritizes action over exploration.

The workspace should therefore feel calmer, more focused and less analytical.

---

# 07. Mission Experience

The Mission is the emotional center of the interface.

It should immediately communicate three things:

* the current objective;
* why it matters;
* what success looks like.

The Mission is never presented as a warning.

It is presented as an opportunity for progression.

Language should remain encouraging, constructive and respectful.

Coach guides.

It never judges.

---

## Mission States

A Mission may be:

* Newly assigned
* In progress
* Near completion
* Completed

Each state should have a distinct visual treatment while preserving visual continuity.

The objective is to create a sense of progression rather than abrupt transitions.

---

# 08. Playbook Experience

The Playbook transforms intention into action.

It should always appear simple.

A trader should feel capable of starting immediately.

Each action should be:

* concise;
* concrete;
* measurable;
* achievable.

Coach avoids overwhelming the user with long lists.

A few well-chosen actions are always preferable to exhaustive guidance.

# **09. Progress Experience**

## Purpose

Progress should make improvement visible.

Not performance.

The objective is to reassure the trader that meaningful change is occurring, even when trading results fluctuate.

Coach therefore celebrates behavioral consistency before financial outcomes.

---

## UX Philosophy

Progress must feel alive.

It should continuously communicate that every disciplined action contributes to long-term growth.

Small improvements deserve visibility.

Large improvements deserve celebration.

The interface should naturally encourage perseverance.

---

## Design Principles

Progress should answer three questions.

> Where am I?

↓

> Am I improving?

↓

> What remains to be done?

Nothing more.

---

# **10. Achievement Experience**

## Purpose

Achievements exist to reinforce positive behaviors.

Never to create competition.

Never to gamify trading.

Their role is psychological.

They acknowledge effort.

They encourage consistency.

They reward transformation.

---

## Recognition Philosophy

Coach celebrates behaviors.

Not profits.

Achievements therefore become memories of personal growth rather than trophies.

When unlocked, an Achievement should feel meaningful.

Not frequent.

Recognition gains value through rarity.

---

## UX Principles

Achievements should surprise.

Not overwhelm.

Their presentation should remain elegant.

Discrete animations.

Premium visual treatment.

No intrusive pop-ups.

Recognition should feel earned.

---

# **11. Digital Twin Experience**

## Purpose

The Digital Twin represents the trader's long-term identity.

Unlike Progress, which evolves daily,

the Digital Twin evolves slowly.

Its role is not to motivate immediate action.

Its role is to answer a deeper question.

> **Who am I becoming?**

---

## UX Philosophy

The Digital Twin should inspire reflection.

It should never resemble a profile page.

It should resemble a living portrait of behavioral evolution.

Every acquired Skill.

Every mastered Habit.

Every long-term strength.

Gradually shapes this identity.

---

## Design Principles

The Digital Twin should feel:

* stable;
* calm;
* timeless;
* aspirational.

It is the quiet memory of the trader's journey.

---

# **12. Empty States**

## Purpose

An empty state is not the absence of content.

It is the beginning of a story.

Coach should never punish a new user with empty containers.

Instead, every empty state should communicate possibility.

---

## Philosophy

Every empty state answers one question.

> **What happens next?**

Examples.

No active Mission.

↓

Coach explains how the first Mission will be generated.

---

No Achievements.

↓

Coach explains that recognition will naturally appear through consistency.

---

No Digital Twin.

↓

Coach explains that identity grows with experience.

---

Empty states teach.

They never apologize.

---

Micro-interactions reinforce confidence.

Not decoration.

Every animation should communicate meaning.

---

## Principles

Transitions should be:

* smooth;
* subtle;
* informative.

Animations must never compete with content.

They simply reinforce state changes.

---

## Examples

Gentle confirmation.

---

Coach adopts the Premium Visual Language established by Dashboard.

## Emotional Palette

The interface should feel:

# 3. Coach Journey Architecture

## Why the Architecture Changed

The first iterations of Coach displayed all Workspaces as a long vertical page.

Although functionally correct, this architecture created an undesirable cognitive effect.

The user immediately perceived Coach as a large collection of information to read rather than an experience to live.

Coach must never feel like documentation.

It must feel like guidance.

For this reason, the module adopts a new interaction model centered around progressive discovery.

The objective is not to reduce the available information.

The objective is to reveal it at the right moment.

---

# Journey Before Information

Coach introduces a new official UX principle inside Cosmos.

> **Journey Before Information**

The product should never expose every piece of information simultaneously.

Instead, it progressively accompanies the trader through a coherent sequence of chapters.

Each chapter naturally prepares the next one.

The user is therefore invited to continue the journey rather than scroll through a long page.

This dramatically reduces cognitive load while increasing engagement.

---

# Coach Journey Container

Coach is no longer composed of five stacked Workspaces.

Instead, it is built around a single persistent container.

This container acts as the permanent workspace of the Coach.

Only one chapter is displayed at any given time.

The container itself never changes its dimensions.

Only its internal content evolves.

This follows the same "Double Background" philosophy introduced by the Journal Wizard while adapting it to a completely different user experience.

---

# Vertical Journey

Unlike the Journal Wizard, Coach does not use horizontal navigation.

The Journal represents a workflow.

Coach represents personal growth.

For this reason, Coach adopts a vertical progression.

Each chapter smoothly leaves through the top of the container while the next chapter enters from the bottom.

This movement symbolises continuous progression rather than step execution.

The interaction therefore reinforces the product philosophy itself.

---

# Chapter Navigation

Navigation remains permanently visible.

The user always knows:

* where they currently are;
* what has already been explored;
* what comes next.

The navigation is composed of two complementary elements.

### Left Column

Displays every chapter.

Example:

```text
Mission

Plan d'action

Progression

Acquis

Portrait
```

The active chapter receives visual emphasis.

The remaining chapters stay visible using a quieter visual treatment.

---

### Right Column

Displays the vertical progression indicator.

Example:

```text
●

○

○

○

○
```

The active indicator smoothly moves as the journey progresses.

It complements the chapter list without competing with it.

---

# Chapter Transition

Only one chapter exists inside the viewport.

When the user chooses to continue:

* the current chapter slides upward;
* the next chapter enters from below;
* the active chapter title updates;
* the progression indicator advances;
* the container itself never moves.

The transition should feel calm, fluid and intentional.

The animation is never decorative.

It communicates progression.

---

# Navigation Philosophy

Coach does not encourage free browsing between disconnected screens.

Instead, it gently guides the trader through a meaningful sequence.

Each chapter answers one question.

Mission

> What should I improve?

↓

Plan d'action

> How should I improve it?

↓

Progression

> Am I moving in the right direction?

↓

Acquis

> Which habits have become part of me?

↓

Portrait

> What kind of trader am I becoming?

The entire experience tells a single story.

---

# Relationship with the Journal

Although both modules use a persistent container, their philosophies differ.

## Journal

Workflow-oriented.

The objective is efficient execution.

Horizontal movement represents procedural progression.

---

## Coach

Journey-oriented.

The objective is personal transformation.

Vertical movement represents continuous growth.

The two modules therefore share the same architectural philosophy while expressing two completely different user experiences.

---

# Expected User Experience

At no point should Coach feel like reading a report.

At every interaction, the user should feel accompanied.

The module should create the impression of progressing through a personal coaching session rather than consulting static information.

The architecture itself therefore becomes part of the coaching experience.
